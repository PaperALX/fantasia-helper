Accuracy Calculator

Notes

Hit rate is an ESTIMATE and may vary wildly in gameplay, especially if less than 5%.

For Warriors

  • 1 DEX = 0.8 accuracy
  • 1 LUK = 0.5 accuracy

For Bowman and Thief

  • 1 DEX = 0.6 accuracy
  • 1 LUK = 0.3 accuracy

For Pirate

  • 1 DEX = 0.9 accuracy
  • 1 LUK = 0.3 accuracy

Magic attack / Magician accuracy is not available in this tool.
